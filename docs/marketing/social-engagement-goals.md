# vedh social and engagement goals

**Status:** active  
**Horizon:** 2026-09-19 through 2026-12-18  
**Purpose:** prove that social distribution can create real Commander play, not merely attention  
**Supporting execution plan:** [TikTok marketing strategy](tiktok-strategy.md)

## Objective

Build a repeatable path from useful Commander content to activated vedh pods while learning which problems, demonstrations, and community relationships produce genuine play.

These targets are operating goals, not forecasts. There is no verified social baseline or complete attribution path yet. The first 20 TikTok posts establish the baseline; they may change the channel mix, but the primary outcome remains activated play.

## North star

**Weekly social-attributed activated pods**

An activated pod is counted when:

1. a host arrives through a tracked social link or a recorded social-assisted referral;
2. the host creates a table;
3. at least one other player joins; and
4. the table records at least ten game actions within 24 hours.

Count a pod once at first activation. Report repeat use separately.

## Definitions

- **Qualified visit:** an external, non-bot visit to a tracked vedh campaign page. Exclude internal and test traffic.
- **Completed signup:** a unique external person who completes account creation. Exclude internal, test, duplicate, and fraudulent accounts.
- **Repeat activation:** an activated host starts another activated table between days 7 and 28 after first activation.
- **Substantive comment:** a question, objection, rules discussion, product observation, or stated use case. Emoji-only and generic comments do not count.
- **Repeatable format:** a content format tested at least three times that produces at least 1.5 times the rolling ten-post median for qualified visits, activated pods, shares, saves, or substantive comments.

## Channel jobs

### TikTok — acquire and demonstrate

- Lead with Commander table friction and visible product proof.
- Publish five times per week in days 1–30, four times per week in days 31–60, and three times per week in days 61–90: 48 posts total.
- Put the product on screen by second three for proof posts.
- Use the tracked `vedh.xyz/tiktok` path and a distinct `utm_content` value for every post.

### X — explain and connect

- Publish twice weekly: one compact product/build note and one proof clip, lesson, or question.
- Use X to reach builders, judges, creators, and rules-heavy players; do not duplicate every TikTok caption verbatim.
- Route product calls to action through tracked links.

### Commander communities — listen and contribute

- Participate only where the contribution is useful without a link.
- Favor product research, rules clarity, board-state examples, and honest build notes over drive-by promotion.
- Treat Reddit, Discord communities, creator circles, and pod conversations as relationship surfaces, not a posting quota.

Do not add another active channel until the TikTok cadence has held for four weeks, weekly production stays within four hours, and the new channel has a distinct job.

## Milestones

### Days 1–30 — establish the baseline

- 20 TikTok posts and 8 X posts
- 100 qualified visits
- 20 completed signups
- 5 activated pods
- 8 substantive user or pod conversations
- 2 candidate formats that beat the rolling ten-post median
- tracked landing route and activation events working end to end

### Days 31–60 — concentrate

- 36 cumulative TikTok posts and 16 cumulative X posts
- 250 cumulative qualified visits
- 50 cumulative completed signups
- 15 cumulative activated pods
- 2 creator or pod collaborations completed
- 70% of TikTok output allocated to the two strongest formats

### Days 61–90 — prove repeatability

- 48 cumulative TikTok posts and 24 cumulative X posts
- 400 cumulative qualified visits
- 80 cumulative completed signups
- 30 cumulative activated pods
- 4+ activated pods in at least 3 of the final 4 weeks
- 30%+ repeat activation among hosts with a complete 28-day measurement window
- 4 cumulative creator or pod collaborations

## Weekly operating cadence

1. Batch source capture and production once per week.
2. Spend 15–20 minutes on real replies after each publication window.
3. Record post metrics at 24 hours and seven days in `tiktok-metrics-template.csv`.
4. Record the cross-channel funnel in `social-weekly-scorecard.csv` and review it for 30 minutes each week.
5. Choose one content adjustment and one funnel or product adjustment for the next week.

Keep the total weekly social workload within four hours. If clicks arrive but activation does not, move time from content volume to the blocked product step.

## Decision rules

- **Remake:** a post produces at least twice the rolling ten-post median for qualified visits, activated pods, shares, saves, or substantive comments.
- **Iterate:** watch, share, or comment quality is strong but tracked visits are weak.
- **Fix the funnel:** visits are strong but signup or activation is weak.
- **Retire:** a format runs three times below the median without useful qualitative learning.
- **Scale a channel:** it produces activated pods for four consecutive weeks without breaking the four-hour workload cap.
- **Stop or reposition:** after 48 TikToks, the channel produces fewer than ten activated pods and no improving qualified-visit-to-activation trend.

## Paid and automation gates

Do not spend on promotion or build custom posting infrastructure until all of the following are true:

- attribution survives from social link through activation;
- at least two organic formats qualify as repeatable;
- at least ten pods have activated;
- repeat activation can be measured; and
- paid tests can optimize for activated pods rather than followers or clicks.

Publishing, rules claims, creator outreach, and community replies remain human-approved.
