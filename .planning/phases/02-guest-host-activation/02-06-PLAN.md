<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 1: Human verification of guest-name curation, the responsive reflow, and the complete logged-out journey</name>
  <precondition>PostgreSQL is running (`make persistence`), the Go server is running (`make run`), and the frontend dev server is running (`npm --prefix app run dev`). `GUEST_CREATION_ENABLED` is unset or `true`.</precondition>
  <what-built>
    Phase 2 in full: the `users` guest columns and the `guestSession` / `refreshGuestSession` /
    `claimGuestAccount` resolvers with their kill switch and rate limit; the curated MTG guest-name
    generator; the public `/play` route and `QuickStartView.vue`; `DeckImportPanel.vue` (now the only
    deck-entry surface in the app, replacing the duplicated CSV textareas in `FormCreateGame.vue` and
    `JoinGameView.vue`); `CommanderReview.vue`; the mobile heads-up notice; the codebase's first
    shared responsive breakpoint; sessionStorage draft survival; the four coded failure classes; the
    server-authoritative `game_created` and `guest_session_created` writes; and the
    `display_name ?? username` render sweep.

    Everything machine-checkable is already green: `go test ./server -race`,
    `npm --prefix app run test`, and `npm --prefix app run type-check` all pass. This checkpoint
    covers only what a machine cannot judge.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    **1. Guest-name curation (D-2.5) — the one genuinely unassertable item.**
    Open `server/guest_names.go` and read the two word slices. Confirm every adjective can sit in
    front of every noun without producing something offensive, slurring, insulting, or absurd. Read
    the full cross product, not a sample — the whole point of a curated list is that its product is
    bounded and reviewable. Flag any pairing you would not want shown to a stranger who just arrived.

    **2. The complete logged-out journey (criterion 1).**
    In a private/incognito window with no `edhgo/auth` in localStorage:
    - Open `http://localhost:5173/play`. Confirm it loads directly with no redirect to `/login` or
      `/signup`, and that the "Paste your decklist" empty state renders.
    - Paste a real 100-card Commander decklist, including at least one deliberately misspelled card.
      Confirm the preview renders totals, the misspelled card appears in the unresolved list with
      suggestion chips, and the nearest match is shown.
    - Apply a suggestion by clicking it. Confirm the totals update and that nothing else in your
      paste changed.
    - Choose a commander in the review step. Confirm an illegal partner pairing is refused with an
      inline message and that a legal single commander enables "Start my table".
    - Leave the display name empty and press "Start my table". Confirm you arrive at `/games/:id`
      and that the board shows you under a generated two-word MTG name.
    - Repeat the run, this time typing a display name. Confirm the board, the games list and the
      score screen all show your typed name.

    **3. Failure recovery (criterion 4).**
    With the run above in progress and a deck pasted, stop the Go server, then press the final CTA.
    Confirm: a product-language failure message with a "Try again" affordance appears, your pasted
    deck, corrections, commander picks and display name are all still on screen, and nothing that
    looks like GraphQL, SQL or a Go error is visible. Restart the server, press "Try again", and
    confirm you reach the board.

    **4. Responsive reflow (D-2.10, D-2.11).**
    With `/play` open, resize the window to 1024px, then 768px, then 375px:
    - At 1024px and 768px: the import surface and the live preview lay out side by side, nothing
      overflows, and the heads-up notice is absent.
    - At 375px: everything stacks in a single column, the page scrolls vertically but never
      horizontally, and the heads-up notice is visible above the import tabs. Confirm the whole
      import flow still works at this width — paste a deck and reach the commander step.

    **5. The authenticated road (criterion 2).**
    Log in as an existing account, then:
    - Open `/play` and complete the same flow. Confirm you are not redirected, and confirm in the
      database that no new row was added to `users`
      (`SELECT count(*) FROM users WHERE is_guest = true` before and after).
    - Open the existing authenticated create-game form. Confirm it now shows the new import panel
      instead of the old "CSV: quantity,name per line" textarea, and that creating a game through it
      still works end to end.
  </how-to-verify>
  <resume-signal>Type "approved" to seal the phase, or describe each issue found (which step, what you saw, what you expected) so it can be routed to gap-closure planning.</resume-signal>
